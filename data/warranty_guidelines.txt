# Company Warranty Claim Guidelines

## 1. Warranty Period

All company products come with a 12-month warranty starting from the date of purchase.

Customers must provide a valid purchase receipt or invoice when making a warranty claim.

## 2. What the Warranty Covers

The warranty covers:

* Manufacturing defects
* Hardware failures caused by normal use
* Faulty internal components
* Product failures that occur during the warranty period

If the problem is confirmed as a manufacturing defect, the company will repair or replace the product free of charge.

## 3. What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not cover:

* Physical damage caused by the customer
* Water damage
* Fire damage
* Damage caused by dropping the product
* Damage caused by incorrect installation
* Unauthorized repairs or modifications
* Normal wear and tear
* Lost or stolen products

## 4. How to Make a Warranty Claim

To make a warranty claim, the customer must:

1. Contact the customer support team.
2. Provide the product name and model number.
3. Provide the purchase receipt or invoice.
4. Describe the problem clearly.
5. Submit photos or videos of the damaged product if requested.
6. Send or bring the product to an authorized service center.

## 5. Warranty Inspection

After receiving the product, the service team will inspect it.

The inspection normally takes between 3 and 5 working days.

The company will check whether the problem is covered under the warranty.

## 6. Repair or Replacement

If the warranty claim is approved, the company may:

* Repair the product
* Replace the faulty part
* Replace the complete product

The company will decide the most suitable option based on the type of defect.

## 7. Rejected Warranty Claims

A warranty claim may be rejected if:

* The warranty period has expired
* The customer cannot provide proof of purchase
* The damage was caused by misuse
* The product was repaired by an unauthorized person
* The product serial number has been removed or damaged

If the claim is rejected, the customer will be informed of the reason.

## 8. Claim Processing Time

Approved warranty claims are normally completed within 7 to 14 working days.

Some repairs may take longer if replacement parts are not available.

## 9. Replacement Product Warranty

If a product is replaced under warranty, the replacement product will continue under the remaining warranty period of the original product.

For example, if the original product has 4 months of warranty remaining, the replacement product will also have 4 months of warranty remaining.

## 10. Customer Support

Customers can contact the support team for warranty assistance.

Support Hours:

Monday to Friday
9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Customers should keep their receipt, invoice, product serial number, and warranty documents in a safe place.
